# Community outcome metric definitions

## Definition scope

The existing `metric-policy.ts` dictionary now names fulfilled needs, event attendance, completed service, saved helpful resources and successful introductions. The authorized Growth dictionary and its authorized CSV export consume the same definitions. Each new entry explicitly says it is not measured in the current report. There are no new counts, source queries, collection, database fields, consent changes, provider calls or public dashboards.

This is a definition contract for later aggregate adapters. Existing operational totals, optional foreground/adoption measurements and their coverage remain unchanged. A source record is evidence of the action it records, not a promise that a stronger real-world outcome happened. Accounts, invitations and participation never measure saved souls, faith or spiritual standing.

This dictionary does not activate a reserved source or grant access to its data.

## Time, correction and coverage

Every future adapter must declare whether it reports a current stock or outcomes completed in a period. Current saved references and current fulfilled needs are stock measures unless their retained canonical history can establish a trustworthy completion boundary. Never substitute `updatedAt`, report time, a due date or an RSVP date for a missing outcome timestamp. Missing historical coverage stays unavailable or partial; it is never backfilled from current state.

For a period measure, use the canonical successful transition after applicable consent and within the reporting interval. Exact retries, repeated saves, edits, duplicate delivery and source aliases add no outcomes. Reopening, correcting, withdrawing, erasing, blocking or losing required authority can restate current-source results. A fresh version or reacceptance does not silently create another person or another completed need. Recompletion policy must be explicit before a flow adapter ships. Snapshot and flow counts cannot be summed together.

Use the existing reporting zone, exclusive interval boundaries, comparison labels and ninety-day optional raw-data limit. Distinct actors and completed units are different measures. Any rate must state its eligible denominator, source coverage and observation window; neither invitations sent nor all registered accounts is an automatic denominator for private outcome rates. No percentage is available with an unknown or suppressed denominator.

## Privacy and authority

The definitions are visible through the existing private Growth report and its separately authorized aggregate export. `VIEW_PLATFORM_METRICS` and `EXPORT_PLATFORM_METRICS` remain separate current checks. No dictionary entry grants access to private participant identities, case text, donor notes, attendance, bookmarks, prayer activity, contact lists or source bodies. A public source does not make its participants' behavior public.

Do not introduce these private outcomes into public dashboards. An internal adapter must still have an approved purpose, lawful/source-specific disclosure and the required explicit choice; the current ordinary-activity choice is not automatic permission to collect new sensitive or private categories. Unsupported church/team scope remains unavailable until its own current authority and aggregation contract exists. No person-level drilldown or faith/health/location breakdown is supplied.

Use the existing minimum group of five distinct eligible people for optional outcome aggregates and detail categories. Count people for this threshold, not events from one person. Suppress complementary cells and unsafe overlapping comparisons when subtraction would reveal a hidden group. Current suppression of operational startup totals is not an exemption for private outcome behavior. Unknown, unsupported, suppressed, immature and measured zero remain distinct. CSV must serialize the same permitted, suppressed projection as the screen.

Withdrawal and account/source lifecycle rules apply before query results, caches or exports. Restored optional consent/facts remain quarantined under the existing recovery policy. A later aggregate adapter needs its own source-boundary, deduplication, small-group/complementary suppression, corrections, retention and export acceptance; this definition change does not claim that implementation.
