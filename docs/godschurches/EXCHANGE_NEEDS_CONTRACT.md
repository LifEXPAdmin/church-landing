## Existing owners and scope

ExchangeListing remains the canonical organization-owned resource. Extend its
existing CHURCH_NEED intent and current church Exchange duties, audience,
publication, photos, search, reports and recovery. Church membership alone does
not grant management. Business delegation retains its existing business-authority
prerequisite. Do not turn ordinary personal NEED posts into church-owned records.

Structured needs may be linked from an authorized church NEED post without
copying their commitments into the post. A linked projection intersects current
post and listing access; removing either source removes the corresponding view.
Reuse current event occurrences and post volunteer roles for timed labor. A need
never creates a second pool of places for an existing volunteer role.

One need coordinates Donate, Sell to us, Transport and Volunteer actions. This
is item/service coordination: no payment processing, deposit, escrow, financial
lending or tax-deductibility representation. A physical equipment loan is
distinct from lending money and retains its return obligation.

## Quantities, proposals and receipts

Use explicit unit labels and bounded positive whole-unit quantities. A slot
names its action, item/help, target and deadline. Keep at most twelve slots per
need, with bounded pages for contributors, current private commitments and
updates. Public counts contain no private contributor IDs, names or notes.

- Donate and Transport: a deliberate claim reserves a quantity atomically.
  A full slot offers a separate explicit waitlist action. A waitlist entry
  reserves nothing and is never silently promoted when capacity becomes free.
- Sell to us: a quote specifies quantity, scope, exact amount and currency.
  It reserves nothing until the current coordinator explicitly accepts it and
  available capacity is checked again. Quote acceptance is coordination, not
  payment or proof of receipt. Never add paid offers to donated-item totals.
- Volunteer: select or configure a current event role through its canonical
  owner. Current volunteer management and participation permissions still apply.
  Capacity, signups and cancellation use PostVolunteerSlot/PostVolunteerSignup.
  Receipt/completion extends that owner rather than mirroring reservation state.

Committed quantity includes the portion still promised and the portion already
received. Received quantity changes only through a current authorized organizer's
explicit confirmation and cannot exceed the supported commitment. A correction
is versioned and audited; reducing a recorded receipt needs a reason. Cancellation
releases only the unreceived portion, once, and preserves the historical promise
and receipt. Concurrent six- and four-unit claims for ten units must total ten;
receiving five of six and canceling the other four must leave truthful quantities.

Slots cannot shrink below retained received quantities plus current unreceived
commitments. Changing the action, unit or underlying volunteer role after
participation requires a new slot, not reinterpretation of old commitments.
An organizer can explicitly close an individual slot, preserving any unmet
amount and reason. Closing a whole need never relabels unreceived promises as
completed help. Completed, partly received, covered, closed partly fulfilled and
canceled states must describe the actual quantities and current availability.

## Time, events and loans

A structured need owns an exact local deadline, time zone and instant. Its
listing date is a projection of that choice; an older listing editor cannot
silently overwrite it. Use the existing time parser and reject ambiguous or
nonexistent local times. A deadline stops fresh claims, not receipt correction,
withdrawal or outstanding loan returns. Event changes and cancellation are
visible from the current canonical occurrence, never a stale copied schedule.

Physical-item loan slots require a return deadline and explicit return
responsibility. Commitments retain those agreed terms. Recording receipt does
not erase a loan return, and closing/canceling a need does not claim the item
was returned. Record actual returns separately; unresolved obligations remain
visible to currently authorized participants without exposing withdrawn source
details. Account erasure and legal preservation follow existing retention owners.

## Coordinator, privacy and recovery

A current church Exchange manager deliberately accepts responsibility for
private offers to the need. Reuse the existing named-receiver authority epoch,
eligible-adult and contact policy. No former appointment, restored membership,
unblock or repeated callback revives earlier consent. Timed volunteer management
continues to require its separate current volunteer duty.

Private offers, quotes, contributor notes and operational details are available
only to their authorized contributor and current responsible coordinator. Other
viewers receive permitted aggregates. Contributor name sharing is a separate,
default-off choice and remains subject to current source/account/block policy.
Authentication email is never a pickup contact. An ordinary manager or moderation
role does not gain an unrelated person's private conversation.

Recheck listing visibility, current church access, participant eligibility,
coordinator authority, membership epochs and bilateral blocks on direct reads,
writes, retained UI and delivery. Revocation must not be undone by later access
restoration. A contributor can withdraw their own remaining commitment without
receiving revoked source details. Preserve honest receipt/loan history while
concealing inaccessible private content. Use the established report preview and
intake owner for deliberately selected evidence and a private dispute flag.

Use current account pinning, the existing permission lock, expected versions and
immutable operation receipts. All capacity changes are atomic. Retry an uncertain
save with the original body and key; do not create another claim or receipt.
Retain unsent entries through network and authentication recovery, conceal them
during account/foreground rechecks and keep deliberate navigation protection.

Protected recovery uses opaque resource/version receipts. Older restored need
or contribution state must be quarantined or restrictively reconciled before
reads, claims, delivery or publication. A stale restore cannot revive a canceled
commitment, old private terms, contributor attribution or coordinator consent.
Include owned data in export and integrate account erasure, retention holds,
source withdrawal and current block handlers.

## Updates and repeat needs

Publish bounded organizer updates with versioned deadline/cancellation notices
to current contributors. Use the existing durable Activity, fanout, outbox and
queue infrastructure. A separate Needs notification category keeps new phone
delivery off until a dated opt-in; older settings forms preserve the new choice.
Notifications remain generic and recheck current source/recipient access,
consent, quiet hours and deduplication. Late contributors do not receive old
notices. Canceling a need stops new commitments and informs its current eligible
contributors. Record actual queue-consumer evidence before release completion.

Repeating a need creates a private draft with descriptions and targets only.
It copies no contributor, commitment, receipt, private note, contact consent,
photo selection, old event signup or completed loan. Require deliberate new
deadlines, coordinator consent and event-role selection before publication.
